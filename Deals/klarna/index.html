      Klarna promo code questions

      What is Klarna's Pay in 4?

      A plan that splits your fare into four equal payments, one due at booking and the rest every two weeks after. It carries no interest.

      Does Klarna charge interest?

      Pay in 4 does not. Monthly financing plans vary: some are interest-free, most are not, and the rate depends on the plan and your approval. Monthly financing is subject to a credit check.

      How do I use a Klarna code on Laters.com?

      Copy the code from the listing above, search your route, and pick a flight. Enter the code in the promo field at checkout, then select Klarna as your payment method.

      What happens when a Klarna offer ends?

      It stops rendering everywhere on the site automatically, including this page, the moment it expires. There is no separate archive of past Klarna codes.

      What if no Klarna code is live when I visit?

      The standing Pay in 4 and monthly financing plans above are always available regardless of whether a promo code is running. A missing code does not mean Klarna itself is unavailable.
